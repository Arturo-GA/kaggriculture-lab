Original experiments add smaller quantities to input-market search, compare
an alternate early-purchase rival scenario, and test finite crop forecasts.
The zero-quantity wheat-pickup fix prevents an empty pickup from blocking the
inherited, cash- and capacity-guarded rescue purchase. Its physical commands
are preserved. Only candidates passing their recorded validation gates are
exported. No private top-player source code is used or inferred from replays.
