# Frontier21 final bounded experiment

Copyright 2026 Arturo-GA. Apache License, Version 2.0.

All inherited source notices are preserved. The only new policy experiment
changes the maximum covered residual sale from 8 units to 12 or 16 units.
Production, food protection, eligible products, similarity filter, sale window,
and number of market orders remain inherited from Frontier20 fill-only.

Selection separates two known development worlds from eight fresh holdout
worlds. If the proposed change does not pass, the user explicitly authorized
the already-tested, tied Frontier20 fill-only agent as the final submission.
Local tests establish no guaranteed leaderboard position or medal.
