The inherited Frontier16, Frontier17 and Frontier18 source and notices remain
intact. Original market experiments search bounded permutations of fully
stocked sales while preserving physical commands, quantities, the relative
order of fixed-price purchases and their earliest positions. Two alternate
selection objectives compare successive responses and the minimum improvement
against two modeled rival queues. Input-quantity experiments and the earlier
zero-quantity pickup correction are separately recorded.

Public episodes were used to analyze observable behavior and to build research
prototypes. No private competitor source was accessed. Rejected production and
forecast prototypes are retained as research evidence, not claimed as gains.
Public replay counterfactuals are diagnostic and do not simulate a reactive
opponent. Local wins are not leaderboard rating predictions.

Release selection: Market2 satisfies the frozen local criteria. Market1 is a
user-authorized exploratory second slot with a recorded two-point regression
against one benchmark opponent. Its failed nonmirror criterion is preserved;
its overall paired score and direct comparison with F18 improved. Functional
cloud checks and exact archive verification are required for both releases.
